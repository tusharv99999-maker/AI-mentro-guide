AI Mentor Guide

A web app that lets a person design their own AI mentor through a short questionnaire, then talk with it. The mentor is inspired by the guidance Krishna gave Arjun in the Bhagavad Gita, and is built to help people who feel lost or unsure of their path.

The mentor is an AI. It does not claim to be a deity, saint, or real person, and it cannot predict anyone's destiny. It is not therapy or professional advice.

Why I'm building this

Many people have a big dream but no visible path. I want to build a warm, honest guide that helps them take one small next step, and encourages them to lean on real people too.

How it works
The user answers 8 plain-language questions that shape the mentor's form, tone, style and foundation.
Answers are turned into a mentor profile.
The profile is combined with fixed safety rules and used to chat.

Safety principles
Always honest about being an AI
Warm but not flattering
Takes mentions of self-harm seriously and points to real help
No medical, legal or financial advice
Encourages real-world connections
